# Event & Competition Gallery Photos

Place photos of hackathons, poster presentations, award ceremonies, and team competition moments in this folder.

Recommended naming:
- `poster-presentation-2024.jpg` (Presenting AI in Education poster)
- `hackathon-team-2026.jpg` (Data Hackathon team working or onstage)
- `codetrap-ceremony.jpg` (CodeTrap award ceremony)

Supported formats: `.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`
